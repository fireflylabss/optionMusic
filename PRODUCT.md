# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience: casual desktop listeners who want an elegant, out-of-the-box local music app — the MacBook-user instinct: open it, see the library, play. They are not chasing streaming discovery; they already own files and want a quiet, powerful player.

Secondary: power users who already know the CLI (`msc` / `optmusic`). The landing speaks to the desktop first; the CLI is proof of depth, not the hero.

## Product Purpose

optMusic (*option music*) is a minimal local music player: a visual desktop that makes the power of the CLI intuitive, plus a terminal player for those who want it. Success for this site is a visitor who feels the product’s calm exclusivity, understands that local music can feel premium without streaming noise, and leaves knowing where to download and get help.

## Positioning

Local music, without the noise — only what matters.

Differentiators that close the gap with classic local players (without streaming cosplay): local playlists + M3U, smart shelves, listening focus, desktop yt-dlp download, pitch/speed, ReplayGain, offline lyrics, reveal + light tag edit — all shared with the CLI engine.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: experience the story of optMusic’s power; conversion is soft — scroll to the end for downloads and help.
- Secondary CTA: GitHub, install paths (desktop / AUR / cargo), docs/help links in the closing section.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: **Música local, sem ruído — só o essencial.**
- Belief ladder: (1) local libraries deserve a premium surface; (2) optMusic is that surface — simple, exclusive, modern; (3) desktop is the intuitive visual form of a serious engine; (4) downloads and help are ready when you are.
- Proof on hand: product itself (CLI + desktop), open source on GitHub, Arch/AUR packaging — no testimonials or press assets yet.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, exacting, exclusive. Apple-like restraint without copying Apple: black-and-white discipline, generous space, one idea per fold. Emotions: calm confidence, ownership, clarity — never hype.

## Anti-references

- Spotify / Apple Music / streaming discovery UIs
- Generic SaaS landings (purple gradients, feature-card grids, “trusted by”)
- Terminal-core / hacker cosplay (mono-only, ASCII-as-brand)
- Generic “premium” with glow, glass stacks, and decorative excess

## Design Principles

1. **Show the power, don’t pitch features** — the page is a thesis about local listening, not a checklist.
2. **One idea per viewport** — Apple-paced scrolls; never crowded folds.
3. **Quiet exclusivity** — premium through restraint, space, and type — not glow or chrome.
4. **Desktop is the face; CLI is the proof** — casual users see the product; depth is earned, not shouted.
5. **Close with clarity** — downloads and help at the end, when the belief is set.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA throughout: contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, and full respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
