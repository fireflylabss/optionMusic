# optionMusic — Local music, without the noise.

A minimal local music player for Linux. Calm desktop UI and a real terminal interface, both powered by libmpv/MPV. No account, no feed, no recommendations — your files stay yours and playback works offline.

## Features

- **Local playlists + M3U** — your lists are files, yours to keep
- **MPV engine, cava spectrum** — serious playback, quiet surface
- **Offline lyrics + ReplayGain** — even volume, no connection needed

## FAQ

### Where does optionMusic look for music?

Your ~/Music folder by default. Point it anywhere with -m / --music-dir.

### Do I need an account?

No. No account, no feed, no recommendations — your files stay yours and playback works offline.

### Which formats can it play?

Whatever MPV plays — MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A, Opus and more, powered by libmpv.

### Desktop or terminal?

Both, same MPV engine underneath. Desktop when you want to see, terminal (msc) when you want to stay.

### How do playlists work?

Local playlists plus M3U — your lists are files, yours to keep.

## Download

On Arch Linux:

```
yay -S optionmusic
```
